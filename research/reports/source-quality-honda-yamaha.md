# Honda and Yamaha source quality

> **NON-PRODUCTION RESEARCH DATA — provenance classification, not a confidence score.**

| Manufacturer | Sources | Official | Service manuals | Owner manuals | Technical/history/spec publications | Catalog-only | Candidate-bearing |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Honda | 20 | 20 | 0 | 0 | 20 | 15 | 5 |
| Yamaha | 15 | 15 | 0 | 0 | 15 | 11 | 4 |

Candidate precision:

- Honda: 70/70 have a named section; 52/70 also retain a page reference.
- Yamaha: 66/66 have a named specification section. No page was asserted because the accessible Yamaha factsheet/web extracts did not provide stable printed-page labels.

The strongest sources in this pass are official Honda UK specification brochures/pages and official Yamaha model factsheets/news-release specification tables. Official histories and line-up announcements are used only for catalogue identity unless they expose a concrete specification table. No community source and no aftermarket catalogue was used for the new Honda/Yamaha candidates.

Absence of service and owner manuals is the principal limitation. A marketing specification table can support displacement, dimensions or tyre sizes, but cannot establish service intervals, clearances, condition-specific capacities or fastener torques.
