# Source access issues

> **NON-PRODUCTION RESEARCH DATA**

- Several official CDN PDF URLs were indexable and their specification tables were accessible to the research tool, but direct PDF opening returned an internal parser error. No page number was asserted for those Yamaha factsheets.
- Honda UK current specification pages are mutable current-model pages. The candidate year and access date are therefore retained, and the page must be archived/re-reviewed before production promotion.
- Honda UK 2021 brochures provided stable printed-page context for the cited specification tables.
- No login-only document, community mirror, infinite redirect or inaccessible service-manual content was used to create new Honda/Yamaha technical candidates.
- URLs must be rechecked during human profile review; `source-located` is not a guarantee of permanent public availability.
