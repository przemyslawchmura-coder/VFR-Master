# Honda and Yamaha catalogue ambiguities

> **NON-PRODUCTION RESEARCH DATA**

- Many historical Honda and Yamaha records have official launch evidence but no single source proving an identical end year in every market.
- Chassis/platform codes are intentionally `null` unless present in the reviewed official material. Generation tokens are proposals, not chassis-code substitutes.
- VFR750/VFR800, FireBlade and YZF-R1 commercial-name continuity spans multiple platforms and regional naming conventions.
- CBR600RR 2020 is Japan-focused while the 2024 record represents the documented European return; they must not be treated as globally interchangeable.
- Honda’s 500 platform spans CB500F/CB500 Hornet, CB500X/NX500 and CBR500R. Shared engine ancestry does not make these one catalogue identity.
- Africa Twin manual/DCT, Adventure Sports and equipment packages remain resolution/applicability concerns unless a future catalogue review proves a separate stable identity is required.
- Yamaha MT-09 Tracer, Tracer 900 and Tracer 9 naming changed while platform ancestry continued.
- Earlier seed keys `yamaha.mt07.rm33.gen3` and `yamaha.mt07.gen4` coexist with broader expanded MT-07 generation proposals. They require human key reconciliation; no automatic alias was added.
- `region: null` means unknown. The region of a source document is not silently promoted to global motorcycle applicability.
- ABS and equipment availability were not used to create extra catalog keys.
