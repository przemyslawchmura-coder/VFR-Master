# Triumph motorcycle catalogue completeness — Wave 2

## Scope and gap audit

Wave 2 deepens the conservative Europe-oriented MY1990–2025 road-production catalogue established in Wave 1. Wave 1 contained 23 families, 85 variants and 480 variant-years. Its family, key, semantic and storedModel/year hashes remain regression boundaries:

## Sources and decisions

The audit used Triumph's [brand timeline](https://www.triumphmotorcycles.com/our-story/brand-timeline), [heritage pages](https://www.triumphmotorcycles.com/our-story), model pages, owner handbooks, press releases, brochures and period technical references. The official 2023–2025 material confirms the Speed 400/Scrambler 400 X production launch, Tiger Sport 800 MY2025 availability and Rocket 3 Storm variants.

Added identities include Street Triple 675/765 R and RS/Moto2, Speed Triple 1050 R/RS and 1200 RR, Speed 400, Scrambler 400 X, Bonneville T100 865, Tiger GT/Rally Pro and Explorer configurations, and Rocket 3 Storm R/GT. Existing names remain aliases where appropriate: T595/955i, Street Twin/Speed Twin 900, Rocket III/Rocket 3, and Tiger Explorer/Tiger 1200 are not falsely merged across architectures.

Tiger Sport 800 is already represented by the Wave 1 2025 record; the official launch announced dealership availability from March 2025. No 2026-only Tiger Sport 800 Tour record is included. Street Triple Moto2 and Daytona Moto2 765 are customer road motorcycles; Moto2 race machines are excluded.

R, RS, RR, XC, XR, GT and Rally variants are separate only for documented chassis, suspension, engine or factory configuration differences. Black, Storm paint, Chrome, anniversary, luggage and other cosmetic/equipment packages remain excluded. No regional emissions or registration-year duplicates were added.

## Result and limitations

The final catalogue has 27 Triumph families, 104 variants and 561 variant-years; global totals are 13 manufacturers, 318 families, 1,095 variants and 5,317 variant-years. No Wave 1 record changed. Remaining gaps include obscure market-only derivatives, detailed Trophy/Thunderbird suffix histories and pre-Hinckley Triumphs outside the schema. This is not a claim of worldwide completeness.
