# Yamaha European Road Catalogue Completeness — Wave 2

## Scope and methodology

This is an evidence-backed, Europe-oriented reconstruction of Yamaha road motorcycles for approximately MY1990–2025. It is not a claim of worldwide or absolute historical completeness. Scooters, mopeds, pure competition machines, grey imports, paint editions, and accessory packages remain outside scope.

The inventory was reconstructed independently by decade, displacement, and product domain. Candidate identities were compared with the 63 pre-wave Yamaha variants. A production split requires a documented platform, engine, fuel-system, chassis, suspension, transmission, electronics, emissions, or discontinuous-return boundary. Direct Yamaha evidence was treated as grade A; official indirect boundary evidence as B; strong historical corroboration as C; unsupported claims as D and withheld.

## Sources inspected

- [Yamaha Motor Europe history](https://www.yamaha-motor.eu/mk/mk/about-us/our-history/yamaha-in-europe/) and national distributor material.
- [Yamaha R-Series pedigree](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/business/mc/lineup/yzf-r/yzf-r7.html), documenting Thunderace/Thundercat context, R1 and R6 redesigns, and the unrelated OW02 and CP2 R7 platforms.
- [Yamaha MT-Series pedigree](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/business/mc/lineup/mt/) and the official [2005 European MT-01 release](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/news/2004/0915/mt01.html).
- [Yamaha XSR-Series pedigree](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/business/mc/lineup/xsr/) and [XSR900 GP launch](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/news/2023/1025/xsr900.html).
- Official European releases for [FZ6/Fazer](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/news/2003/0821/fz6.html), [FZ6 S2](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/news/2006/1010/fz6-fazer.html), [XT660R](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/news/2003/0909/xt660r.html), [FJR1300/FZS1000](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/news/2000/0913/intermot.html), and [XJ6 Diversion](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/news/2008/0909/xj6s.html).
- Yamaha Communication Plaza collections and period Yamaha News product-range issues, including the official 1993 range containing GTS1000/GTS1000A.
- Official owner/service manuals, parts catalogues, brochures, homologation records, and Yamaha Europe price lists for year and parallel-version corroboration.
- Strong period road tests and established historical catalogues were used only to corroborate older European boundaries where Yamaha's online archive is incomplete.

## Boundaries, overlaps, and remaining gaps

Legitimate parallel variants include YZF750 R/SP, FJ1200/ABS, FZ/Fazer bodies, MT SP, XSR900 GP, Tracer GT/GT+, FJR A/AE/AS, XT660R/X, XT1200Z/ZE, Ténéré derivatives, WR R/X, XV950/R, and XJ6 bodies. Cosmetic anniversary and paint editions were not added.

Preserved discontinuities include VMAX 2004–2008, SR road singles between the original European range and 2014 return, and the existing European R6 end after MY2020. OW02 and CP2 R7, MT-03 660 and 321, and VMAX 1200 and 1700 remain unrelated identities.

Remaining gaps include consistent R1M/R1 SP boundaries, Fazer 125, national-market Royal Star/Venture ranges, SCR950/XTribute scope, early XV national availability, and country-specific low-volume two-strokes. These require stronger European primary evidence. Scooter families were inspected but deliberately excluded because the production catalogue is motorcycle-oriented.

All family IDs, variant IDs, and global keys are unique. StoredModel/year collisions are zero. Intentional overlaps are technically distinct. All 63 original Yamaha semantic identities are unchanged; no compatibility correction was required.
