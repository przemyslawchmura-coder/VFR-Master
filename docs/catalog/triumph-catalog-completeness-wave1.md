# Triumph motorcycle catalogue completeness — Wave 1

## Scope and baseline

This Wave 1 catalogue is a conservative, Europe-oriented inventory of Triumph road motorcycles for model years 1990–2025. It is not a claim of worldwide completeness. Before the work Triumph had 9 families, 38 variants and 186 variant-years; those records were frozen and remain unchanged.

## Method and sources

Research used Triumph's [official brand timeline](https://www.triumphmotorcycles.com/our-story/brand-timeline), [heritage pages](https://www.triumphmotorcycles.com/our-story), current model pages, owner handbooks and press/media material, with period brochures and reputable motorcycle references used to cross-check model-year boundaries. The official timeline records the 1994 Speed Triple/Tiger 900 launch, 1996 Daytona T595, the 2000 Bonneville return and the 2015 liquid-cooled Bonneville family; the official model pages describe the Tiger 900 GT/Rally split and current [Speed Twin 900](https://www.triumphmotorcycles.com/motorcycles/classic/speed/speed-twin-900). Exact ranges are intentionally conservative where markets differed.

## Coverage and identity decisions

Wave 1 adds the early Hinckley modular range (Trophy, Trident, Sprint, classic Daytona), Thunderbird/cruiser lines, Legend/Adventurer, TT600/Speed Four, and modern classic families (Speedmaster, Bobber, Thruxton, Speed Twin). Tiger road/adventure configurations are represented separately where XC/XR or GT/Rally chassis and equipment are meaningful; cosmetic packages are not duplicated.

* T595 and Daytona 955i are one continuing 955-generation road identity (the canonical stored name is “Daytona T595 / 955i”), not two motorcycles.
* Street Twin and Speed Twin 900 are a single 900cc liquid-cooled Bonneville-platform identity; the later name is an alias/marketing transition.
* Tiger 800 and the 2020 Tiger 900 are separate generations; Explorer is retained as the pre-2018 name while the later Tiger 1200 generations are distinct.
* Rocket III and the 2020 Rocket 3 are separate engine/chassis generations. Classic, Touring and Roadster are included because they are factory configurations with materially different chassis/bodywork.
* R/RS/RR/XC/XR/GT/Rally suffixes are separate only when they identify a documented factory chassis or engine configuration. Paint, Black, anniversary, Chrome and accessory editions are excluded.

## Exclusions and limitations

Race-only Moto2 machinery, prototypes, concepts, authority derivatives and cosmetic special editions are excluded. Daytona Moto2 765 is included only as the customer road-production homologation model. North-American emissions labels and registration-year differences do not create variants. The catalogue still needs a later audit of obscure regional derivatives, early pre-Hinckley Triumphs, and some limited homologation editions.

## Result

The implementation contains 23 Triumph families, 85 variants and 480 variant-years. All pre-existing keys, storedModel/year identities and semantic records remain stable. Global catalogue totals are 13 manufacturers, 314 families, 1,076 variants and 5,236 variant-years. Validation covers key/semantic/collision checks, generation boundaries, legacy resolution and regression protection for all previously completed manufacturers.
