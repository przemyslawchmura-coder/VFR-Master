# Suzuki European Catalogue Completeness — Wave 2

## Scope and method

This wave reconstructs a practical Europe-oriented Suzuki production catalogue for MY1990–2025. It covers road motorcycles, road-legal dual-sport and supermoto models, and Suzuki's established European Burgman/Address scooter lines. It does not claim worldwide completeness. Pure competition motorcycles, cosmetic editions, concept vehicles, grey imports, and models supported only by another market were withheld.

The audit started with all 63 production Suzuki variants and compared them with Suzuki ranges by decade, displacement, and product domain. A variant split requires a distinct platform, displacement, chassis/model identity, material configuration, or documented generation boundary. Names alone are insufficient. Exact year ranges were accepted from official launch/history material, manuals and parts catalogues, European homologation/range evidence, or corroborating established references; uncertain endpoints were narrowed or excluded.

## Sources inspected

Primary Suzuki sources included:

- [Suzuki Digital Library](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/motorcycle/smgs/digital-archive/), including dated GSX-R, TL, Bandit, DR-Z, Intruder, Address, Burgman and other production milestones.
- [GSX-R 40th Anniversary history](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/motorcycle/smgs/products/gsx-r_40th_anniversary/) and Suzuki's GSX-R production history.
- Global Suzuki launch material for the [2017 GSX-R1000/R, GSX-R125, GSX-S750 and V-Strom updates](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2016/1004.html), [GSX-S125, GSX250R and Burgman 400](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2016/1108.html), and [SV650X](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2017/1107.html).
- Official launches for the [2020 V-Strom 1050/XT](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2019/1106.html), [2021 GSX-S1000](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2021/0426.html), [2023 V-Strom 800DE and GSX-8S](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2022/1108a.html), and [2025 DR-Z4S/DR-Z4SM](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2024/1105.html).
- Official European material for the [2012 V-Strom 650](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2011/0627a.html), [2013 Burgman 650](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2012/1003.html), [Burgman 125/200](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2013/0924b.html), and [2023 Burgman Street 125EX/Address 125](https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2022/1004a.html).
- Suzuki owner/workshop manuals, parts catalogues, European brochures and national distributor archives, including Suzuki Austria's official owner-manual library, for model codes and older year boundaries.

Older European ranges with incomplete online manufacturer archives were cross-checked against period road tests, established model histories, parts/application catalogues, and homologation/model-code references. A single marketplace listing was never treated as generation evidence.

## Coverage added

### Sport and sport touring

- `gsx-r1100`: three oil-/water-cooled European ranges, MY1990–1998.
- `gsx600f`, `gsx750f`, `gsx650f`, and `gsx1100f`: distinct displacement lineages, not a generic GSX-F bucket.
- `rf`: RF600R and RF900R.
- `tl1000`: parallel TL1000S and TL1000R identities.
- `b-king`, `gsx250r`, and shared-platform `gsx125` for GSX-S125/GSX-R125.
- GSX-R1000R added alongside the existing base L7 identity.

### Roadsters and current road range

- `gs500`, `gsx1100g`, `inazuma`, `gsx1400`, and `vx800` restore important 1990s–2000s standards.
- `gsx-s950`, `gsx-s1000f`, `gsx-s1000gx`, and `gsx1250fa` extend the modern and sport-touring range.
- SV650X and GSX-S1000GT+ are retained as parallel, materially configured production variants rather than paint editions.

### Adventure, trail, and supermoto

- `freewind`, `dr125`, `dr350`, `dr650`, `dr-big`, `dr-z400`, and `vanvan` cover defensible European road-legal ranges.
- DR-Z4S and DR-Z4SM are separate MY2025 identities because Suzuki launched them as distinct dual-purpose and supermoto models.
- V-Strom 1000XT, 1050XT, and 1050DE are parallel equipment identities; existing base V-Strom records remain untouched.

### Cruiser and classic

- `intruder-vs`, `intruder-vl`, and `intruder-m` preserve recognizable European Suzuki naming and platform groups.
- `marauder`, `savage`, `gn125`, and `en125` cover established learner, commuter, and cruiser lines.

### Scooters

- `burgman-125-200`, `burgman-250`, `burgman-400`, and `burgman-650` use documented platform/generation breaks.
- `address` includes the older European Address 125, the later Address 110, and the MY2023 European Address 125 return.
- Skywave and Boulevard are treated as regional aliases, not duplicate European identities. Burgman Street 125EX remains under Burgman; Avenis is withheld pending a broader small-scooter scope decision.

## Regional naming decisions

- European `Intruder` names are canonical; North American `Boulevard` names are aliases and do not create duplicate variants.
- `Burgman` is canonical for Europe; Japanese `Skywave` names do not create separate families.
- GSX600F/GSX750F are not duplicated under the North American Katana marketing name.
- GW250 is stored as `GW250 Inazuma`, the established European name combination.
- Existing `DL1050 V-Strom 2023` remains the stable base identity; the DE configuration has its own stored model.

## Researched but excluded or unresolved

- RM, RM-Z, DR-Z400E, and other closed-course competition models: researched, but excluded from the owner-oriented road catalogue.
- GSX-R400, Bandit 250/400, SV400, GS1200SS, and modern Japanese GSX250/400 derivatives: official European production-range evidence was insufficient; grey/JDM presence is not enough.
- Boulevard-only C/M/S variants and US-market DR650SE continuation: not imported into Europe as a uniform range.
- TU250X, Volty, Grasstracker, Djebel, Goose, and other market-limited standards/trails: unresolved European scope.
- Avenis, Katana 125, Burgman 150 and additional small scooters: country/range boundaries need a separate scooter audit.
- Special editions, anniversary liveries, ABS-only labels without another service identity, and accessory packages were not split.
