# Honda European Road Catalogue Completeness — Wave 2

## Scope and method

This wave reconstructs Honda’s Europe-oriented road-motorcycle range for approximately MY1990–2025, compares it with the existing production catalogue, and adds defensible missing identities. Scooters, pure competition motorcycles, cosmetic editions, and models supported only by JDM/US/Australian evidence remain outside production.

The audit worked from Honda’s documented range rather than a supplied model shortlist. Families were inventoried across sport, roadster, touring, adventure/crossover, road-legal trail, cruiser/custom, and general-road domains. Generation splits require a documented chassis/platform, fuel-system, displacement, emissions, or materially service-relevant equipment change.

## Sources inspected

Primary Honda sources included:

- [Honda CBR model history](https://global.honda/en/CBR/models/) and individual CBR stories.
- [Honda CB history](https://global.honda/en/CB/history/), including the CB1100 European-launch and evolution histories.
- [Honda CRF history](https://global.honda/en/CRF/), which identifies CRF250L, CRF250 Rally, and European CRF300L milestones.
- Honda Global newsroom European model announcements for [MY2006](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2005/c050930a-eng.html), [MY2007](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/worldnews/2006/2061010Motorcycle-Models.html), [MY2008](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2007/2071001-eng.html), [MY2014](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2013/2131105eng.html), [MY2024](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2023/c231107aeng.html), and [MY2025](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/news/2024/c241105beng.html).
- Honda’s VFR history and European VFR800X/VFR1200X publications, including the [2016 Crosstourer release](https://global.honda/en/newsroom/worldnews/2015/2151022VFR1200X-Crosstourer.html).
- Honda owner/service manuals, European parts catalogues/microfiche, brochures, and homologation/model-code references for older models.

Older European boundaries were corroborated with period road tests and established historical references. The VFR400R NC30 was admitted narrowly for MY1990–1991 because official UK and limited continental European imports are independently documented; later JDM production was not included. Marketplace listings were not used as generation evidence.

## Duplicate, overlap, and compatibility audit

All family IDs, local variant IDs, and global variant keys are unique. All years are four-digit integers and all ranges are ordered. Stored-model/year collisions are zero. Parallel Fireblade SP, Africa Twin Adventure Sports, CBR600F Sport, CBF N/S, and CB1100 EX/RS records use distinct stored identities because their service-relevant equipment differs.

All 96 pre-wave Honda variants were compared against the pre-wave catalogue and remain semantically unchanged. No existing correction or compatibility migration was required.

## Remaining gaps and uncertain boundaries

- VTR250 MY2009 needs stronger Honda Europe archive evidence before production inclusion.
- DCT/manual transmission variants are not yet split consistently across legacy Honda families. Doing this safely requires a catalogue-wide compatibility plan because several stable identities currently cover both transmissions.
- ABS/non-ABS subvariants remain combined unless Honda catalogued them as a materially separate identity.
- Road-legal XR availability varied by national homologation; a country-specific audit is needed.
- RC213V-S has European evidence but is a tiny homologation special; its fit with the practical owner catalogue remains unresolved.
- Older VT/VTX/Shadow derivatives require country-by-country European range evidence before further splitting.
- CB1300 continuation outside the recorded European sales ranges must not be inferred from Japan-market production.

No Technical Profile, source registry, resolver, search, clarification, UI/store, Supabase module, or research candidate changed in this wave.
