# Kawasaki Family Structure Audit

## Compatibility rule

Family names are user-facing; `variant.key` and `storedModel` are stable identity surfaces. All 64 pre-Wave-2 Kawasaki records therefore retain their original family, local ID, key, name, stored model, and year range. New families improve structure without turning cleanup into a migration.

## Existing families reviewed

| Existing family | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| `ninja-250-500` | Keep: successive European lightweight Ninja platforms are explicit variants and all are protected identities. |
| `z-small` | Keep: Z300/Z400/Z500 are successive lightweight naked platforms; changing the family would break the protected baseline. |
| `er-6` / `ninja-650` | Keep separate: European ER-6f naming precedes the distinct later Ninja 650 catalogue lineage. |
| `h2` | Keep: Ninja H2, H2 SX, and Z H2 share the supercharged technology family but remain explicit variants. |
| `versys-1000-1100` | Keep: the 1100 is the documented successor in the same large Versys lineage. |
| `gtr1400` | Keep; add older 1000GTR separately because it is a distinct platform. |
| ZX-6R/ZX-10R families | Keep displacement-specific supersport families; add missing historical ZX lineages similarly. |

## New structure

- ZX and ZZR families are displacement/lineage-specific rather than one broad Ninja bucket.
- GPZ500S, GPZ900R, and GPZ1100 remain separate because GPZ branding spans unrelated platforms.
- `ninja-1000sx` preserves the European Z1000SX to Ninja 1000SX rename in one continuous lineage.
- `ninja-z125`, `electric`, and `hybrid` group explicitly shared platforms while keeping body/configuration variants distinct.
- `zrx`, `zephyr`, `z900rs`, and `w` use durable recognizable classic/standard lineages.
- KLE, KLR, KLX/D-Tracker, and Versys-X remain separate to avoid an arbitrary adventure bucket.
- VN/Vulcan families are divided by related displacement/platform generations; regional Vulcan naming is not duplicated.
- ZL/EL historical and current Eliminator models use one recognizable `eliminator` family with explicit variants.

## Result

Thirty natural families are added, taking Kawasaki from 17 to 47 families. No existing family is renamed, split, merged, or reassigned. All 64 original semantic identities survive; 68 additions produce 132 variants and 647 variant-years. No key, semantic identity, storedModel/year pair, or pre-existing variant is duplicated or lost.
